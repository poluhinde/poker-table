# Classic jumbo-index card faces

Source: [SVGCards by Saul Spatz](https://github.com/saulspatz/SVGCards),
`Decks/Vertical2/svgs`, commit `f8df28774736ea2545fc8e7fb693eb55ce031945`.

The author dedicates the graphics to the public domain. The SVG metadata identifies
CC0. Original court-card artwork is credited to Byron Knoll. The upstream README,
including the dedication and acknowledgements, is retained in `SOURCE.md`.

Files were renamed to the application's card codes (for example,
`heartQueen.svg` → `QH.svg`). The indices were redrawn at a smaller size, the central
artwork was enlarged uniformly, and the inner frames were removed. Original court
illustrations are preserved. No Copag logos or artwork are included.
